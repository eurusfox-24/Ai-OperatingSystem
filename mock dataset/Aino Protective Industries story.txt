Aino Protective Industries began in a small workshop near Tampere, Finland. The company was founded in 2016 by Aino Lehtinen, a materials engineer who had previously designed protective equipment for construction workers. She believed that safety gear should protect people without becoming excessively heavy or uncomfortable.

The idea for the company developed after Aino met a rescue worker who complained that traditional protective vests were difficult to wear during long emergency operations. Aino gathered a small team of engineers, textile specialists, and product designers to create a lighter and more flexible bullet-resistant vest.

Their first product was called the Aino Shield One. It used an advanced material named FlexWeave, made from tightly layered synthetic fibres and lightweight ceramic plates. The vest was designed for police officers, security workers, emergency responders, and journalists working in high-risk environments.

Developing the vest was challenging. During the first year, several prototypes were too heavy, while others became uncomfortable in cold weather. The company spent months testing different fabrics, shapes, ventilation systems, and adjustable straps. Aino insisted that every design should pass strict safety and comfort inspections before reaching customers.

In 2018, Aino Protective Industries received its first major order from a private security company in Northern Europe. The order included 500 vests with removable protective panels and personalised identification labels. This contract allowed the company to move into a larger factory and hire 35 new employees.

The factory used automated cutting machines to prepare fabric sections accurately. Important stages such as stitching, inspection, and final fitting were completed by trained workers. Every vest received a unique serial number, manufacturing date, and digital inspection record.

Aino later introduced the ArcticGuard model, designed for cold climates. It included moisture-resistant fabric, insulated shoulder sections, and adjustable side panels that could be worn over winter clothing. The company also created a lighter model called CityFlex for workers who needed protection while remaining mobile.

By 2024, Aino Protective Industries employed 140 people and supplied protective equipment to customers in 18 countries. The company opened a research centre where engineers studied stronger materials, improved comfort, and environmentally responsible manufacturing methods.

Despite the company’s growth, Aino continued visiting the production floor every Friday. She spoke with workers, reviewed customer feedback, and examined new prototypes.

A sign above the factory entrance displayed the company’s guiding message: “Protection should give people confidence, not fear.”

For Aino, the company was not only about manufacturing protective vests. It was about helping people return home safely after completing difficult and dangerous work.
